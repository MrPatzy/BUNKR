                    Mesh check-ins (MeshCore)

                    The radio

                    A MeshCore companion radio on this machine: plugged into USB (a Pi: /dev/ttyACM0 or /dev/ttyUSB0), or a Heltec on Wi-Fi companion firmware (tcp:192.168.1.60:5000). BUNKR puts the channels below in their slots and listens on them. It needs MeshCore's companion firmware (USB or Wi-Fi), not repeater or room-server firmware.

                    Channels

                    Paste a channel's key (or the meshcore:// link the MeshCore app shares) to use a channel you already have, or leave the key empty and BUNKR makes one. Slots are only for this radio; phones can keep theirs.

                    The bot

                    Commands anyone on a channel can use (WHO, PING and ? always answer). Alerts to the mesh are set per channel above: flash alerts go out at any hour, the rest wait out 22:00-06:30, at most 6 an hour; reminders never go.

                    With radios on the hub and the Pi, let only one of them send calls and "needs help", or the family hears them twice.

                                  Radios

                                  Print all channel cards Reference sheet

                                  Each radio gets a channel list, a CHIRP file (in CHIRP: File, Import, then Radio, Upload to radio), a printable channel card, and a history of programming images you save from CHIRP. All of it is copied to the Pi and laptop, so a radio can be reprogrammed with no internet.

                                  Channels

                                  The master plan: a slot number means the same channel on every radio. Radios with a model profile are built from it automatically; a radio that can't use a channel leaves that slot empty.

                                  Standard set: GMRS/FRS 1-22 and the GMRS repeater channels, NOAA weather 1-7 (receive only), and the 2 m and 70 cm calling frequencies. Tick "In family plan" for the channels everyone should know.

                                  Mesh firmware (offline)

                                  Firmware files kept in the downloads store for your boards. Fetch them on the Downloads tab (admins); the Pi and laptop carry copies and have the flashing tools installed.

                                  Important documents

                                  Register files or folders (scans of IDs, policies, deeds, medical records). They're copied to the USB kit in an encrypted 7-Zip archive, highest priority first. Nothing is moved or changed in place.

                                  With a date, BUNKR reminds you 30 days before, then every week once it's overdue, until it's marked done on the maintenance list or given a new date.

                                  Node lock
                                  
      
                                  A Pi or laptop with node lock keeps the family plan, inventory and check-ins encrypted, so a stolen one
        gives away nothing. At home it unlocks by itself from this hub. Away from home, someone types this passphrase on the
        node's page.                                                   How to upgrade
            
                                                    
              
                                                  1. Back up first (a snapshot of the machine or container, or at least /var/lib/bunkr).
                                                    2. 
              
                                                  2. Update the BUNKR folder you installed from: git pull in it, or copy in the new release's files.
                                                    3. 
              
                                                  3. From that folder, run the installer again with this machine's role, for example
                sudo ./deploy/install.sh --role hub (or --role pi, --role laptop). Add --dry-run first to see what it will do.
                                                    4. 
              
                                                  4. The database updates itself when BUNKR starts. Your data, keys and settings stay. Upgrade the hub first, then each node.
                                                    5. 
              
                                                  5. Full steps: "Upgrading" in deploy/INSTALL.md.
                                                    6. 
            
                                                  
            
                                                  Nothing changes until you do this. You can stay on this version as long as you like.

        the flight's own times, delays and gates, and pushes when it's 30+ minutes late. The Personal tier includes about $5 of
        lookups a month at no charge; BUNKR stops at a monthly cap well inside that.
